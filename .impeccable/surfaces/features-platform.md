Scope: Rideshare platform pages (features/platform/**), Operate mode. Audience: dispatchers, fleet and finance staff, and drivers working their own trips.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The platform reads like a licensed cab's papers: requests and trips set against the supply that can take them, identifiers set like plates, the stage of every trip on its rail. It refuses the category default of a filtered list under a pill status track.
OWN-WORLD: Cab yellow (oklch 0.86 0.165 92) with plate-black text only for the primary action, and a deeper yellow rule for the current tab; pale plate white for table heads; square status marks; radii 3-6px; tabular, slashed-zero counts and times.
STORY: The dispatcher sees how many are waiting, the oldest wait and who is available, then offers a ride from the row; managers keep drivers, vehicles, services and money right.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and live line; a ruled supply strip (Waiting, Oldest, Available, Offered, Assigned, On trip, plus who is available now); underline status tabs; the request rows with route line, wait time and the offer control.
FORM: Licence plate and hackney medallion, candidate 6 of 7 (dispatch T-card board, radio log, taxi-rank signage, meter receipt, street atlas, licence plate, destination blind); seed:fb289b52ba31cf0b. Raises: columns that hold a crowd in one gaze (split-flap board); rank by weight and rule at one size (timetable slide rack); the line you are on burns brightest (transit map).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
