Scope: Rideshare rider pages (features/storefront/**, app/page.tsx, app/book, app/rides, app/account), Persuade mode. Audience: a rider booking a local ride on a phone.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Booking a ride is getting a meter slip before the ride: where from, where to, which car, and the fare in big figures, on the kerb at night. It refuses the generic ride-hailing clone of rounded cards, pill toggles and glowing CTAs.
OWN-WORLD: Asphalt ground, kerb-stone surfaces, signal-yellow edges for the chosen option and the one primary action, thermal-white slips for the quote, square 3px corners, tabular figures for fares and times.
STORY: The rider names pickup and destination, picks a service, sees a bound price with its expiry and fees, and requests the ride; later they follow it and review their rides.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The service name, "Where to?", pickup and destination fields joined by the route line, the service choices as square kerb options, and the signal-yellow "See price".
FORM: Taximeter slip, candidate 4 of 7 (roof light, door livery, airport kerb signage, meter slip, street atlas, coach ticket, taxi-stand neon); seed:9d243b1c9d7cae26. Raises: needles that lean together on the same downbeat, so the fare, expiry and fees read as one reading (VU-meter bridge); spot colour used with total restraint (risograph system).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
